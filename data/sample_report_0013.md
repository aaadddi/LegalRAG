# Incident Report INC-0013

On 2026-06-08 at approximately 14:30, employee Sofia Alvarez filed a noise complaint originating from Warehouse A, Aisle 12. She stated the noise was disruptive to her assigned tasks and had persisted for over 45 minutes. Security and operations management were notified to investigate the source and implement corrective measures.
