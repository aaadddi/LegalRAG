# Incident Report INC-0034

On 2026-05-30, an equipment malfunction was reported at Warehouse B, Loading Dock 3. Employee Grace Okafor observed the automated pallet wrapper experiencing a critical error, ceasing operation mid-cycle and displaying an "E-05 Power Fault" diagnostic. The unit became inoperable, preventing the processing of outbound shipments from that dock. Grace Okafor immediately powered down the machine and notified facilities maintenance. No injuries or further property damage occurred; repair technicians are en route.
