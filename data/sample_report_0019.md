# Incident Report INC-0019

On 2025-11-22, James Whitfield attempted to gain unauthorized access to Parking Structure C. Security footage observed Mr. Whitfield attempting to tailgate another vehicle through the controlled entry barrier. Security personnel, Marcus Reyes, intervened and identified Mr. Whitfield, who confirmed his access badge was inactive. Mr. Whitfield was escorted off the premises and advised against further attempts to enter company property without authorization. This incident is being reviewed for potential security policy violations.
