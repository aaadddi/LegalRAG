# Incident Report INC-0039

On 2026-05-25, Lena Fischer reported a sustained noise disturbance originating from Warehouse A, Aisle 12. Ms. Fischer described the noise as a loud, rhythmic clanking affecting her work area. Security personnel investigated the complaint and confirmed an intermittent mechanical noise from equipment near the specified aisle. The source was identified as a vibrating pallet jack not in active use. The equipment was subsequently secured, and the noise subsided.
