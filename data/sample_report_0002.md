# Incident Report INC-0002

On March 2, 2026, at approximately 09:45, a coffee machine in the 2nd
floor break room was found damaged. Priya Nataraj (Administrative
Assistant) reported the damage to David Chen (Floor Supervisor), who
filed this incident report. Initial assessment suggests the machine's
water reservoir was cracked, possibly due to mishandling. No witnesses
were identified. Facilities was notified for repair. Chen noted this is
the second maintenance issue reported in this break room in the past
quarter.
