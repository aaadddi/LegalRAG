# Incident Report INC-0031

On 2025-09-27, James Whitfield bypassed the front reception desk, gaining unauthorized entry to the main office area without presenting proper credentials or scanning an access badge. The incident was observed by onsite security personnel via CCTV footage. Whitfield proceeded approximately 15 feet into the restricted zone before being approached. He was subsequently escorted back to the reception area for identification and questioning. Access logs are currently being reviewed to determine the full extent of the unauthorized access.
