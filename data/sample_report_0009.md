# Incident Report INC-0009

On 2025-11-29, at approximately 09:15 AM, the primary visitor check-in kiosk at Front Reception experienced a critical software error, freezing and becoming unresponsive. Grace Okafor, a Front Desk Associate, observed the malfunction and immediately placed an "out of order" sign on the unit. James Whitfield, a software engineer, was passing by and attempted a hard reboot, but the system remained inoperable. A priority IT service ticket was subsequently opened for hardware and software diagnostics.
