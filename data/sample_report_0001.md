# Incident Report INC-0001

At approximately 14:20 on January 14, 2026, a verbal altercation occurred
between Marcus Reyes (Forklift Operator, Shift B) and David Chen (Floor
Supervisor) near Loading Dock 3. Witnesses report the dispute began over
a delayed shipment schedule. Reyes raised his voice and struck a nearby
pallet with an open hand. No physical contact was made between the two
individuals. Chen filed a verbal complaint with HR the same day. Reyes
was issued a verbal warning per company policy section 4.2. No further
action was taken at this time. Security camera footage from Dock 3 (Unit
7) was reviewed and corroborates witness statements.
