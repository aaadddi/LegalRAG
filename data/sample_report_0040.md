# Incident Report INC-0040

On 2025-11-05, at approximately 14:30, property damage was reported in Warehouse A, Aisle 12. Employee Sofia Alvarez observed Tomas Kowalski operating a forklift when it made contact with a storage racking unit. The impact caused structural damage to the racking and dislodged a pallet of packaged server components onto the floor. No injuries were reported, and the affected area was promptly secured pending further investigation and repair.
