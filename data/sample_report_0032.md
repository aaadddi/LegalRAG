# Incident Report INC-0032

On 2026-04-06, an equipment malfunction was reported at Warehouse B, Loading Dock 3, involving the automated pallet wrapper system. Operators Lena Fischer and Grace Okafor observed the machine unexpectedly stopping and emitting a burning odor during routine operation. The incident caused a temporary disruption of outgoing shipments from that dock. Power to the unit was immediately cut, and the area was secured by personnel. No injuries or further damage were reported.
