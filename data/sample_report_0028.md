# Incident Report INC-0028

On 2026-01-25, Grace Okafor was observed leaving a portable device charger cable extended across a main walkway in the Front Reception area. Sofia Alvarez subsequently tripped over the unsecured cable but regained her balance without falling or sustaining injury. This created a clear tripping hazard, violating established workplace safety protocols for public areas. The incident was documented for review by HR and Facilities, with immediate corrective action taken to secure the cable.
