# Incident Report INC-0030

On 2025-09-07, employee Sofia Alvarez reported a theft from her personal vehicle, parked on Level 3 of Parking Structure C, sometime between 08:00 and 17:00. Ms. Alvarez stated that a backpack containing personal electronics and identification was stolen from the passenger seat. Security Officer Omar Haddad responded to the scene, documented the damage to the vehicle's rear passenger window, and initiated a review of relevant CCTV footage. Ms. Alvarez was advised to file a formal police report and was provided with the incident reference number.
