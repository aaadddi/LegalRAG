# Incident Report INC-0010

On 2026-02-07, Lena Fischer reported a noise complaint originating from Warehouse B, specifically Loading Dock 3. Ms. Fischer described intermittent loud impacts disrupting work in adjacent areas. Security personnel investigated the complaint and observed a forklift operating at Loading Dock 3, causing significant noise when moving large palletized items. The forklift operator was advised to exercise caution and minimize impacts during operations. Noise levels were subsequently observed to decrease following this intervention.
