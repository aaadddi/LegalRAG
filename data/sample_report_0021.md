# Incident Report INC-0021

On 2026-03-03, an individual identified as Angela Brooks attempted to proceed past the Front Reception desk into a restricted employee area without proper access credentials or visitor registration. Lena Fischer, a Front Desk Administrator, observed the unauthorized entry attempt and immediately intervened. Fischer directed Brooks back to the main lobby, informing her of the required check-in procedures. Brooks complied without further incident, and the situation was de-escalated. Security protocols were reinforced with reception staff.
